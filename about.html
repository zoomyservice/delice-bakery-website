About

Our bakery

Your little corner of France in Los Angeles.

Located in the heart of Pico-Robertson, Delice Bakery is a kosher French bakery and café dedicated to the art of fine baking. Our passion is rooted in tradition — every croissant, baguette and tart is made from scratch, using authentic techniques and the highest-quality ingredients.

From golden, flaky viennoiseries to decadent layer cakes and delicate entremets, our creations are inspired by the elegance of French patisserie. We also serve a selection of fresh sandwiches, crisp salads and hot dishes — perfect for a light lunch or for catering your special event.

At Delice Bakery, baking is more than our craft — it's our way of bringing people together. Whether you're here for your morning espresso, a Shabbat dessert or a festive celebration, we welcome you with the warmth and hospitality that have made us a neighborhood favorite.

Come for the flavor, stay for the experience — Delice Bakery is your little corner of France in Los Angeles.

A family-owned French bakery, pastry shop & café in Pico-Robertson. 100% kosher — Pas Yisroel, Cholov Yisroel and Yoshon.

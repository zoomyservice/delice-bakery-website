About

Our Bakery

Delice Bakery is a fine French bakery, pastry store & restaurant located in Los Angeles — family owned and 100% kosher: Pas Yisroel, Cholov Yisroel, and we use only Yoshon flour (except in our muffins).

We offer fresh artisan breads and home-made pastries & cakes, but also an array of healthy salads & sandwiches, and so much more.
